The loop: BUILD your base (2 minutes) → WAR: break enemy cores → CHAOS: sudden death and disasters. Last team standing wins; on timeout the best score wins.

Cores: every base has a glowing purple core. While yours stands you respawn 5 s after dying. Enemies must mine it (slow) or blow it up; cores are shielded for the first minute of the war. When CHAOS starts every core crumbles: from then on, death is final.

Maps: Plains, Canyon (cliffs) and Village (houses). Weapons: sword, axe (slow, heavy), bow, knockback staff. Water and lava buckets are in the inventory too.

Multiplayer: host a game, share the code or invite link, pick a map, ready up. If a player disconnects, AI takes over their character.

Resources are finite. Mine trees, stone, iron and dirt. TNT, water, lava and machine parts are rare: spend them where they hurt most.

Mechanisms: a pressure plate or tripwire powers any TNT, piston, door or alarm touching it, directly or through wire. Doors copy the block they are built against.

Plot walls: tall walls hide every plot during BUILD. They fall when the war starts.

Machines (E menu): Arrow Turret (right-click to refill), TNT Cannon (uses your TNT), Land Mine, Spike Trap, Flamethrower, Launch Pad, Conveyor, Sprinkler, Healing Beacon and TNT Dropper (wire it). They only hurt enemies, and only once the war starts.

Combat: like Minecraft: charge each swing, hit while falling for a critical, sprint into a hit for extra knockback, and a short immunity follows every hit.

Physics: blocks need support. Cut the base of a tower and it falls, crushing what is below. Sand and gravel fall. Water flows, lava burns, fire spreads on wood.

Weapon skins (Locker): buy skins for your sword, axe, pickaxe and bow with coins you earn by playing. Each gives that weapon a small boost.
