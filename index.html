The loop: BUILD your base → PREPARE traps → FIGHT → DESTROY enemy bases → SURVIVE → CHAOS disasters. Last team standing wins; on timeout the best score wins.

Resources are finite. Mine trees, stone, iron and dirt. TNT, water, lava and machine parts are rare: spend them where they hurt most.

Mechanisms: a pressure plate or tripwire powers any TNT, piston, door or alarm touching it, directly or through wire. Doors copy the block they are built against.

Physics: blocks need support. Cut the base of a tower and it falls, crushing what is below. Sand and gravel fall. Water flows, lava burns, fire spreads on wood.

Base integrity: your base counts only blocks you built around your spawn. Below 5% it is destroyed.
